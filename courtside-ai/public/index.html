How a point gets scored

No referee, no clipboard. The players call it, the tablet checks it, the desk sees it.

Assign from the desk

Queue the draw, tap a court, and the tablet on that fence shows the names and who serves first. Rules are global and versioned; change them once and every live court adopts them.

Say the score before you serve

Server first, English or French. The tablet reconciles instead of obeying: a call that is one point away is applied, the same score is a confirmation, anything else is rejected with a one-tap fix.

It syncs, even when the Wi-Fi does not

Every change is saved on the tablet and queued in an outbox. Cut the link and the court keeps scoring; reconnect and the desk catches up in order. Changeovers run the sponsor loop and call “Time”.
